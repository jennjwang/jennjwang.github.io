Jennifer Wang

Ph.D. student in Computer Science, Stanford University

I study how AI is changing work and how to build AI systems that enhance human expertise. I'm advised by Diyi Yang and Erik Brynjolfsson and supported by an NSF Graduate Research Fellowship. Right now I am building TaskNet, a next-generation occupational task inventory.

Before Stanford, I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Brown University, advised by Suresh Venkatasubramanian. Previously, I worked on AI policy at the White House OSTP, Brookings, and Mozilla.
